Hi, I'm a Ph.D. student at the Faculty of Science, Charles University in Prague. I work in the Department of Theoretical Chemistry of the Heyrovský Institute, Czech Academy of Sciences, as a member of Jiří Pittner's group.

My research focuses on machine learning for nonadiabatic molecular dynamics. I develop ML models for excited-state potential energy surfaces and the couplings between them, which make surface hopping simulations of photochemical processes faster and allow them to reach longer timescales.

Research

I study ultrafast dynamics of molecules and clusters including nonadiabatic and spin–orbit effects. If you would like to learn more about my research or projects, feel free to reach out!

Machine learning for excited states

Models for potential energy surfaces, nonadiabatic and spin–orbit couplings that make long simulations affordable.

Nonadiabatic dynamics

Surface hopping simulations of what molecules do after absorbing light, including intersystem crossing.

Software

I contribute to the Newton-X and MLatom packages.

Other topics of our group include quantum chemical methods for strongly correlated systems (multireference and DMRG-based externally corrected coupled cluster methods) and quantum computer algorithms for quantum chemistry.
